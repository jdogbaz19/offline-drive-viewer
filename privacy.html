Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Offline Drive Viewer catalogs your drives, folders and cloud accounts so you can browse and search them when they aren't connected. This policy explains what the app stores and where.

In short: we don't collect any of your data. Everything stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account.

What the app stores

To build a catalog, the app reads the drives, folders and cloud accounts you choose to add, and records:

  • File and folder names, paths, sizes, dates and kinds
  • Media details such as dimensions, duration, codecs, audio tags and small thumbnails
  • Text from documents, PDFs and subtitles, if you turn on content search
  • Scan history, drive health readings, backup jobs, saved searches and the location notes you write

The app only reads the locations you add. It never uploads the contents of your files anywhere.

Where it's kept

  • On your devices. The catalog is a database inside the app's own container on each device.
  • In your iCloud account. If you're signed in to iCloud, catalogs, thumbnails, saved searches and connection status sync through your private iCloud database, so your other Macs and your iPhone can show them. Apple stores this data under your Apple Account. We, the developer, can't access it. Text indexed for content search, drive health, and backup jobs aren't synced; they stay on the Mac that made them.

Cloud accounts (Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Drive)

When you add a cloud account, you sign in on that service's own page. The app gets an access token, which is stored in your device's keychain and never leaves that device. The app uses the token to list your files and fetch thumbnails directly from the service. Nothing passes through any server of ours. You can remove the source in the app to delete its token, and you can revoke the app's access in your account settings at Dropbox, Microsoft or Google at any time.

Read-only access is all the app asks for: file details (and thumbnails) from Dropbox, Files.Read and your basic profile from OneDrive, and file metadata from Google Drive. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Spotlight

If you turn on Spotlight indexing, names and details of cataloged files are added to the system's on-device Spotlight index so you can find them from Spotlight. The index stays on that device.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, crash reporting services, advertising or tracking
  • No accounts with us, and no servers of our own
  • We don't sell or share any data, because we never receive any

Deleting your data

Removing a source in the app deletes its catalog, thumbnails and tokens on that device and removes it from your iCloud database. Deleting the app removes its local data. To remove synced data, manage your iCloud storage in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage, or System Settings on the Mac.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
