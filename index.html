Offline Drive Viewer

Catalog your drives and folders on your Mac, then browse and search them anywhere, even when the drive is in a drawer.

  • Every drive, one placeExternal drives, Mac folders, iCloud Drive and Time Machine.
  • Find it fastSearch names, kinds, sizes, dates and text inside documents across all your drives.
  • On your iPhoneCatalogs sync through your own iCloud, so you can check what's on a drive from anywhere.
  • Keep it safeFind duplicates, compare folders, watch drive health and run backups.
